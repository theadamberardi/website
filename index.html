AI Workflow Assessment and Implementation

My consulting engagement helps your institution evaluate an existing process, identify appropriate uses of AI, redesign the workflow, and pilot the process with strict validation procedures.

Assess

Understand the current workflow, pain points, inputs, outputs, and institutional responsibilities.

Design

Identify appropriate AI opportunities and redesign the process around clear standards and human responsibilities.

Implement

Develop the structured prompts, processes, validation mechanisms, and AI-supported workflow.

Validate

Test the workflow, evaluate results, document the procedure, and establish ongoing human oversight.

Grounded in Real Higher Education Work

My approach is grounded in applied practice. In a recent AI-enabled program review reporting project:

  • The system was developed specifically for a higher education program review reporting process.
  • It incorporated standardized inputs, structured prompts, validation, and human oversight.
  • Report production time was reduced by approximately 50%.
  • The approach was successfully used across 14 program reports.
  • The workflow shifted effort away from repetitive report construction toward validation, interpretation, analysis, and decision support.
